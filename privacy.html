Privacy

Gearso collects no data about you. That is the whole policy; the rest says how.

What we collect

Nothing. Gearso has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It sends nothing about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (repaired machines, stars, journal pages) and your settings are saved on your device only, in an encrypted database. Reset progress in the game's settings clears your progress. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

Sheets are bought through the store you installed Gearso from (the App Store or Google Play), under that store's own privacy policy. The store tells the game which sheets you own. We never see your name, your payment details or your account.

Children

Gearso collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If Gearso ever starts handling data, this page will say so before it does.
